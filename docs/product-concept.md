# Product Concept

> 思いついた言葉を投げるだけで、自分の言葉と温度を保ったまま蓄積され、必要な時に過去のアイデアと再会できる場所

この一文をContextual Memoryの判断基準とする。

## Product principles

- Capture must be easier than organizing.
- Preserve the original text without modification.
- Produce an edited version only by removing redundancy and obvious verbal clutter.
- Preserve the user's wording, first person, emotion, hesitation, momentum, and metaphors.
- Keep AI interpretation separate from the saved memo.
- Resurface memories through current relevance instead of requiring manual review.
- Do not turn the product into a task manager, folder hierarchy, or status workflow.
